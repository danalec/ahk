# The 0.6.5: what the release carries, and the gaps that are left

The record of the release **0.6.5** of the tree. It says what the release
carries, what was measured, and what the tree does not carry yet.

The record of the dialect of the v2.0.32 is in
[`v2.0.32-changes.md`](v2.0.32-changes.md), and the instrument that says what
the tree does not carry is in
[`corpus/v2-corpus-gaps.md`](corpus/v2-corpus-gaps.md).

## What the release carries

The release is the sum of the commits between the 0.6.4 and it, and they
are of three kinds.

**The refusals of the v2 that the tree now shares.** The corpus of the
upstream carries 1745 scripts, and 45 of them were scripts that the
official v2.0.32 refused at load time while the tree loaded them. All 45
are refused by the tree as well:

- an end-of-line `;` comment after whitespace interrupts an unclosed
  quoted literal (14);
- the name of a class of the script is not the output variable of an
  assignment, and the name of a function of the machine is not either (19);
- a hotkey is not allowed inside a function or a class (4);
- a call does not take more parameters than the function takes, and a
  parameter that is required is not left out (3);
- the bare `IsSet`, the unary `&` on something that is not a variable, the
  `catch` of a class that is shadowed or unknown, the block of a hotstring
  that opens outside the mode `T`/`R`, and the helpers that
  `Any.Prototype` carries (5).

**The v1.** The v1 had sixty-eight gaps against the pairs of the upstream
and it has **zero**. They are closed family by family: the name of a label
that carries any character, the options of the hotstring, the commands that
turn the lock keys, the remap, the arity of the comparisons, the seven
functions that the table of the surface did not carry, and the rest -- the
table of every one of them is in
[`corpus/v2-corpus-gaps.md`](corpus/v2-corpus-gaps.md).

**The pairs of the upstream.** The instrument that fixes the UTF-8 BOM and
the newline at the end of the file of the probe moved 67 artefacts of the
corpus into "both load", and uncovered three gaps of the v1 that were also
fixed: the `Process, Priority` whose level is a single letter or
`Realtime`, the `SendRaw`, and the `Hotkey, IfWin*` whose second parameter
is a window and not a label.

## What was measured

Every number of this release is a measurement against the binary of the
official release, and every one of them can be run again:

| The instrument | What it asks | The answer |
|---|---|---|
| the corpus of the upstream (`scripts/corpus-check.py`) | does the script load, in the tree and in the official v2.0.32? | **0** gaps in 1745 scripts |
| the pairs of the upstream (`scripts/pairs-check.py`) | the same, for the 851 pairs of the v1 and of the v2 | **0** gaps of the v1, **0** gaps of the v2 |
| the differential harness (`scripts/differential.py`) | does the real binary and the tree answer the same? | **114 of 114** MATCH |

## The gaps that are left

There are none of the kind that the tree refuses a script that the official
loads. What is left are the two lists of the other kind, and they are the
rules that need a file or a library that the machine does not have:

- **488** of the corpus ask for a `v2.1-alpha` that the version of the
  tree does not carry, and **35** name an `#Include` of a library that is
  not there;
- **2** of the pairs of the v1 and **98** of the pairs of the v2 are
  `#Include` or `#Requires` refusals of the same kind.

The table of every one of them, with the numbers and the reasons, is in
[`corpus/v2-corpus-gaps.md`](corpus/v2-corpus-gaps.md).
