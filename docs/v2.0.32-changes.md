# The v2.0.32: what the release changed, and what the tree does about it

The record of the move of the tree from the dialect of AutoHotkey v2.0.31 to
the dialect of the **v2.0.32**. It is a record, not a guide: it says what was
measured, what the two releases answer, and what the tree carries.

The v2.0.32 was published on the 20th of November of 2026, and it is the
stable that the tree speaks now. The record of the move from the v2.0.19 is in
`docs/v2.0.31-changes.md`.

## The method

Two binaries were compared:

| The release | The binary | The digest |
|---|---|---|
| 2.0.31 | `%TEMP%\ahk-v2-measure-2.0.31\AutoHotkey64.exe` | `ca1a595aca4528755d8d4cd7fed23d497a37bd9a0b6892072612d68dee1bd00a` |
| 2.0.32 | `%TEMP%\ahk-v2-measure-2.0.32\AutoHotkey64.exe` | `384a4c125e8d7b5b77ddbd5a593ec761d3e9e96e97a95931cf56e9a077fda114` |

The digests are the ones that the release page of the upstream publishes, and
they were checked before the binaries were unpacked. The measurement of the
surface was made with `scripts/measure-surface-2.0.32.py`, and the behaviour
was measured with the scripts of `scripts/` and with the two MCP servers of
`../ahk-v2-headless` (the vanilla v2.0.32 with the headless build on top of
it, so that the answers come out structured instead of in a dialog).

## The surface did not move

| What | 2.0.31 | 2.0.32 |
|---|---|---|
| The names of the surface | 843 entries | **843 entries** |
| The names that are new | -- | **none** |
| The names that are gone | -- | **none** |
| The arities that changed | -- | **none** |
| The arity that the questions measured | 345 names of 345 | **345 names of 345** |

The release is a release of fixes, and the fixes do not touch the names and
do not touch the arities. The table of the tree moved from
`ahk-v2.0.31.surface.tsv` to `ahk-v2.0.32.surface.tsv`, and the rows of it are
the same rows: the measurement of the v2.0.32 and the table of the v2.0.31
have the same 844 rows, with the same names, the same kinds and the same
arities. Only the addresses of the handlers moved, because they are the
addresses of another build.

## What the release changed

The upstream published eight fixes. The tree carries the one of them that a
script sees at the load, and the other seven do not change what a script of
the tree answers.

### The options of a hotstring are checked at the load — carried

Measured on the binary of the v2.0.32: an option of a hotstring that the v2
does not know is refused at the load with "Invalid option." and the text that
is from it to the end of the options. The v2.0.31 ignored it.

The options of a hotstring are the `*`, the `?`, the `B`, the `C`, the `O`, the
`R`, the `T`, the `X` and the `Z`, each of them with a `0` or a `1` after it;
the `K` and the `P` with a number, that may have a sign; and the `S` with an
`I`, an `E`, a `P`, a `0` or a `1`. A space and a tab are ignored, and the
scan stops at a colon.

| The script | The v2.0.31 | The v2.0.32 | The tree |
|---|---|---|---|
| `#Hotstring Y` | loads | "Invalid option." / "Y" | **"Invalid option." / "Y"** |
| `#Hotstring RndChars` | loads | "Invalid option." / "ndChars" | **"Invalid option." / "ndChars"** |
| `#Hotstring EndCharsX` | loads | "Invalid option." / "EndCharsX" | **"Invalid option." / "EndCharsX"** |
| `:Y:btw::by the way` | loads | "Invalid option." / "Y:btw" | **"Invalid option." / "Y:btw"** |
| `#Hotstring C` | loads | loads | loads |
| `#Hotstring EndChars \`,` | loads | loads | loads |

The `R` of the `RndChars` is an option of a hotstring, and the `n` that
follows it is not, so the refusal names the text that is from the `n`.

The tree carries it in three places: the directive `#Hotstring` (in the
lexer), the definition of a hotstring of the script (in the parser) and the
command `Hotstring` (at the run). The helper is
`the_options_of_a_hotstring_are_valid` in `crates/ahk/src/hotkeys.rs`.

### The end characters of the hotstrings are a word of their own — carried

Measured on the binary of the v2.0.32: the `EndChars` and the characters that
follow it are the end characters of the hotstrings, and the `EndCharsX` is an
option that the v2 does not know. The v2.0.31 took the `EndChars` as a
substring of the name of the option.

### The `S` option of a hotstring does not swallow the next character — not observable in the tree

Measured on the binary of the v2.0.32: the `S` with a sub-letter of `I`, `E`
or `P` is one option, and the `*` that follows it is the option of the raw
text. The v2.0.31 took the `*` as the sub-letter of the `S` and lost it.

The tree keeps the options of a hotstring but does not type anything with
them: a hotstring of the tree calls a function of the script. The fix
therefore does not change what the tree answers.

### The hotkey of the hook may be turned on and off — not carried

Measured on the binary of the v2.0.32: the `Hotkey "AltTab", "On"` and the
`Toggle` of it work again; the v2.0.31 broke them in the v2.0.13.

The tree has no hotkey of the hook: the `Hotkey "AltTab", "On"` is refused
with "Parameter #2 of Hotkey is invalid.", which is the tree's own reason and
not the one of the binary ("Nonexistent hotkey.").

### The `Format` of a malformed specifier — the answers are the same

Measured on the binary of the v2.0.32: a specifier that runs to the end of the
string is not read past the end of it any more.

The tree answers the three forms that were measured the same way the two
binaries answer them: the `{0:.123`, the `{0:.9999999999999999999999999}` and
the `{0:123456789012345678901234567890.5}` are left as they were written.

### The RegEx of an invalid UTF-16 — not carried

Measured on the binary of the v2.0.32: the RegEx of a string that holds an
unpaired surrogate is refused with "PCRE execution error." and does not
crash the process.

The tree cannot hold the input: its strings are UTF-8, and an unpaired
surrogate is not a text of it. The `RegExMatch(Chr(0xD800), "a")` answers
without a failure where the binary refuses.

### The debugger — not carried

Three of the eight fixes are of the debugger: the freeze when the client
disconnects without a `detach`, the `source` command that does not stop at the
end of the file when the `-e` is left out, and the corruption of the large
values that `property_value` answers.

The tree has no debugger. The `OutputDebug` of the v2 writes to the log of the
machine, and the tree keeps the log.

## The measurement of the tree

With the binary of the v2.0.32 as the reference, the tree answers the 113
cases of `scripts/differential/cases` the same way the binary does:

```text
cases: 113  engines: real, rust
MATCH   113
DIFFERS 0
ERROR   0
```

The `A_AhkVersion` of the tree is the `2.0.32` now, and the version of the
tree is what the scripts of the corpus compare against: the corpus of
`../AHKv2_Finetune` asks for the `v2.1-alpha.16` in 716 of its 1745 scripts,
and the tree refuses them the way the v2.0.32 refuses them.
