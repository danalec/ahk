# The 0.6.4: what the release carries, and the gaps that are left

The record of the release **0.6.4** of the tree. It says what the release
carries, what was measured, and what the tree does not carry yet.

The record of the dialect of the v2.0.32 is in
[`v2.0.32-changes.md`](v2.0.32-changes.md), and the instrument that says what
the tree does not carry is in
[`corpus/v2-corpus-gaps.md`](corpus/v2-corpus-gaps.md).

## What the release carries

The release is the sum of the commits between the 0.6.3 and it, and they are
of three kinds.

**The name of the v2.** The name of a variable, of a function and of a
label of the v2 carries any character that is not ASCII: the `★a★b★c()`
is a function that the official loads, and the `_1★a★b★c:` is a label. The
upstream says the same in its `IS_IDENTIFIER_CHAR`: a character above the
ASCII is one.

**The name that the script builds.** The name of a property of the literal
of an object may be built by the script -- the `{key%var%: 1}` is the
property "keyC" when the value of `var` is "C". A word of the language
that is followed by a percent sign is the beginning of a name that the
script builds, and not an operator: the `is%mytype%(3)` is the call of
the name "isInteger". And the text that is glued after the closing
percent is a part of the name: the `%prefix%_dynamic` is the variable
whose name is the value of `prefix` and the "_dynamic".

**The hotkey of the v2.** At the load, any single character is the name
of a key, whatever the character is: the `{::`, the `(::` and the `}::`
are hotkeys that the official loads, and the `@` and the `\` are
characters of the name of a hotkey. The line that begins with three
colons is the hotkey whose name is the ":", and not a hotstring.

**The v1.** The name of a label of the v1 is the text that comes before
the colon, and it may carry any character but a space and a comma. The
hotstring of the v1 may carry the options of the v2. The commands that
turn the lock keys of the keyboard, that write the text of a control and
that undo the minimize of every window are carried. The declaration of
the v1 carries the equal sign -- the `static var = 1` is a declaration
with the value one -- and the `#Delimiter` of the v1 changes the
character that separates the parameters of a command.

## What was measured

Every number of this release is a measurement against the binary of the
official release, and every one of them can be run again:

| The instrument | What it asks | The answer |
|---|---|---|
| the corpus of the upstream (`scripts/corpus-check.py`) | does the script load, in the tree and in the official v2.0.32? | **0** gaps in 1745 scripts |
| the pairs of the upstream (`scripts/pairs-check.py`) | the same, for the 851 pairs of the v1 and of the v2 | **0** gaps of the v2, **43** of the v1 |
| the differential harness (`scripts/differential.py`) | does the real binary and the tree answer the same? | **114 of 114** MATCH |

## The gaps that are left

They are two lists, and they are of two kinds.

**The forty-three gaps of the v1**, out of the 851 pairs of the upstream, by
the shape of the refusal: the call of a function that the v1 has and the
tree does not ("Call to nonexistent function." in nine), the command of the
v1 that the tree does not carry ("This line does not contain a recognized
action." in five), and twenty-nine more of fifteen other shapes.

**The forty-five gaps of the other kind**, out of the 1745 scripts of the
corpus: the tree loads a script that the official v2.0.32 refuses at the
load. They are the rules of the v2 that the tree does not carry yet -- the
name of a class of the script is not the output variable of an assignment
(12), the name of a function of the machine is not either (7), a hotkey is
not allowed inside a function (4), a call does not take more parameters than
the function takes (3), and the other nineteen of five other shapes.

The table of every one of them, with the numbers and the reasons, is in
[`corpus/v2-corpus-gaps.md`](corpus/v2-corpus-gaps.md).
