# The 0.6.7: the compiled path, and the window that the script found last

The record of the release **0.6.7** of the tree. It says what the release
carries, what was measured, and what the tree does not carry yet.

The record of the dialect of the v2.0.32 is in
[`v2.0.32-changes.md`](v2.0.32-changes.md), and the instrument that says what
the tree does not carry is in
[`corpus/v2-corpus-gaps.md`](corpus/v2-corpus-gaps.md).

## What the release carries

The release is the sum of the commits between the 0.6.6 and it, and they are
of six kinds.

**The loop of the tree is compiled, and the call of the script is in it.**
The tree interprets the tree of the script at the run; the official compiles
it. The release moves the paths that every script walks into the compiled
path: the loop of the integers, of the floats and of the registers compiles
onto flat slots, and the row of the built-in is resolved when the loop is
compiled. A call whose name is shadowed is not compiled -- a function of the
script with that name wins over the built-in.

The call of a **function** of the script is in the compiled path too, and its
arguments are read from the registers of the loop: the value of a variable
of the loop is not in the script while the loop runs, and the generic path
reads the script. Measured with the fine clock, the `func_call` answers in
**73 ms** where it took 131 ms, and the official takes 78 ms; the phase
`phase_func_one` goes from 406 ms to **156 ms**, and the official takes
156 ms.

The call of a **method** of a class of the script is in the compiled path:
the name of the method is the text of the source, and it is read where it is
written; the target of the call is the value of an expression of the loop;
and the arguments are the ones that read nothing. Measured with the fine
clock, the `method_call` answers in **56 ms** where it took 104 ms, and the
official takes 62 ms.

The `NumGet` and the `NumPut` of a loop are in the compiled path as well: the
type of the number is the text of the source, and it is resolved when the
loop is compiled. Caching the backing buffer cell across the turns of the
compiled `NumPut`, skipping the lookups of the pointer of a `NumPut` whose
address is discarded, and decoding the bytes with `from_le_bytes` brought
the `numput_numget` from 141 ms down to **47 ms**, against 110-140 ms of the
official (`0,34x`-`0,43x`).

And the **text of a value is shared, and not copied**: every evaluation of a
literal and every write of a variable copied the text of it. The official
shares it -- the value of a text is a pointer to the text of the script -- and
its copy is free.

**The call that names no window asks about the window that the script found
last.** It is the rule of the two dialects, and it is in the source of the
official:

- the v2.0.32, `window.cpp:490-494`: *"User passed no params, so use the
  window most recently found by WinExist()."*
- the v1.1.37.02, `script2.cpp:10911-10923`: *"Use the 'last found' window."*

Only the `WinExist` puts a window there. The title `A` is the window that has
the focus, and `WinActive()` is the window that has the focus only when it is
the one that was found last. The tree kept **no** window at all and answered
with the window that was in the foreground of the machine -- the document
said so, and the document was wrong. The tree keeps one now, and it answers
about it the way the v2 answers.

The `WinGetID` stopped memorizing it: the official does not, and a tree that
answers more than the official is a tree that a script cannot rely on. The
type of the `WinGetID` and of the `WinGetIDLast` is `Integer`, and not the
text of a handle.

**The window of another program is read the way the official reads it.** The
`WinGetText` of the v2 reads the texts of the **child windows** of the
window that the call names (the v2.0.32, `lib/win.cpp:2075`: the
`EnumChildWindows` of the target). The tree read the window of the script
and refused every window of another program. The desktop answers it by the
identifier of the window now, and a desktop that cannot tell the bar of the
title of a program from its content refuses **with that reason**.

The `WinGetControls` and the `WinGetControlsHwnd` read the controls of the
window of the machine, and the values are **exactly** the ones of the
official: the same `ClassNN`
(`Windows.UI.Composition.DesktopWindowContentBridge1`,
`Windows.UI.Input.InputSite.WindowClass1`) and the same handles (524328 and
131270).

**The v1 answers the way the binary answers.** The sweep of the loads asks
the binary of the v1.1.37.02 and the tree the same line of the v1 -- every
command of the table of the reference with zero to five parameters -- and
puts the two complaints side by side. The sweep found the family that was not
carried, and the release closes it: the name that the script builds and that
is blank is refused the way the v1 refuses it, the three commands that go on
fail with the `ErrorLevel` of the machine and not with a stop (the
`SplashTextOff` with no splash, the `URLDownloadToFile` of a url that is not
one, and the `WinMenuSelectItem` of a window that is not there), the
`KeyWait` waits for the key, the `ControlSendRaw` is refused like the
`ControlSend`, the handle of a window is a hexadecimal text, the `OnExit`
names a label or is a function, the `SetTimer` without a parameter does not
kill the script, the `FileOpen` answers zero when the file is not there, the
`Hotstring` wants its definition, the COM answers the empty text where the v2
throws, the `LV_*` and the `TV_*` are silent when the script has no such
control, and the fourth parameter of the `RegExMatch` of the v1 is not a
number.

The v1 also has the command `WinWaitNotActive` now, and the catalogue of a
dialect carries the family of the names that no family claims.

**The instruments do not move the machine.** The sweeps used to drag the
mouse of the machine and to leave the sound of it on: they run thousands of
scripts, and every one of them can drive the machine. They do neither now.
The gates find the reference binaries where the machine keeps them (the
v2.0.32 headless build and the v1.1.37.02 of the release) and they do not
write in the tree. The arity of the v2 and the target of the family of the
windows are measured **on the binary**, and not on the table of the tree --
a table that the tree writes is a table that cannot disagree with the tree.
The reader of the sweep finds the dialog of a binary that relaunches itself.

**The refusals of the tree say what the official says.** The pairs of the
upstream are 851 programs of the v1 and the same programs of the v2, and
the harness asks the tree and the two official interpreters the same
question: does the script load? The gate counts a pair as a gap when the
tree refuses a script that the official loads, and there is **none** of
them. The words of the refusal were not a part of that count, and a probe
that measures them found **ten** pairs that refuse with different words.

Six of the ten are closed:

- the declaration that the dialect refuses is named **as the script wrote
  it**: the `static MODULEID := 230909, oldNotify="*"` is refused with the
  comma and the spaces of it, and the text of the tokens is not that text.
  The v1 names the declaration without the newline in it -- the "Static x"
  and the ", y := 1" are the "Static x, y := 1" -- and the v2 names it
  with a space where the newline was;
- the name of a built-in is the output variable of a statement **only**: the
  `:=` of a statement is the output variable of it, and the same `:=` inside
  a list or inside the arguments of a call is an expression. The refusal
  says "This Func cannot be used as an output variable." in the first case
  and "This Func cannot be assigned a value." in the second, and the
  compound operator is never the output variable of a statement.

The four that are left are measured, and each one says what it is:

- the line of the v1 that is a name that the script builds is refused by
  the official -- "This line does not contain a recognized action.
  Specifically: %A_LoopRegKey%\\%A_LoopRegSubKey%" -- and the tree loads
  it;
- the v2 goes on after the refusal of a line and names the last one, and
  the tree stops at the first: the "Accumulated Errors 01" of the upstream
  is refused by the official at the declaration of the seventh line, and by
  the tree at the literal of the third;
- the call of the v1 that carries a continuation section is named by the
  official with the text of the section, and the tree names it with the
  placeholder of it.

**The floor of the Windows XP is measured again.** The tree takes
`user32.dll!GetAncestor` to the machine now -- it is the walk of the
parent that finds the window of the top level that holds the control under
the pointer -- and the floor of the XP is an oracle of three answers: the
symbol that was measured missing, the symbol that was measured present,
and the symbol that nobody asked about. The gate fails on the third, and
the forty-one symbols that the work of the GUI had added were recorded by
hand, with a note that the probe was to run again.

The probe ran on the machine of the lab on the 25th of November: **424
symbols asked, 424 present, none missing**. The `GetAncestor` is with
them, and the oracle of the tree is measured and not guessed.

## What was measured

Every number of this release is a measurement against the binary of the
official release, and every one of them can be run again.

The speed of the tree, against the official v2.0.32 (release build, the
median of the runs of the instrument `scripts/perf-compare.py`):

| The workload | The tree | The official v2.0.32 | The ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `arr_push_get` | 109 ms | 218 ms | 0,50x |
| `func_call` | 93 ms | 109 ms | 0,85x |
| `map_set_get` | 219 ms | 313 ms | 0,70x |
| `method_call` | 78 ms | 94 ms | 0,83x |
| `noop_loop` | 16 ms | 31 ms | 0,52x |
| `numput_numget` | 47 ms | 140 ms | 0,34x |
| `obj_prop` | 31 ms | 63 ms | 0,49x |
| `strings` | 78 ms | 63 ms | 1,24x |
| `while_loop` | 172 ms | 250 ms | 0,69x |

The tree is faster in **eight of the nine**: the compiled path of the tree is
as fast as the official on the loops of the language, and the one that is
behind is the text of the arguments of a built-in, which the tree copies and
the official does not.

The gates of the tree, each of them run against the binary of the official:

| The instrument | What it asks | The answer |
|---|---|---|
| the differential harness (`scripts/differential.py`) | does the real binary and the tree answer the same? | **119 of 119** MATCH, 0 regressions |
| the pairs of the upstream (`scripts/pairs-check.py`) | does the same program answer the same in the v1 and in the v2? | **0** gaps of the v1, **0** gaps of the v2 |
| the words of the refusal of the pairs (`scripts/pairs-words.py`) | do the tree and the official refuse with the same words? | **135** pairs refuse the same, **4** do not |
| the corpus of the upstream (`scripts/corpus-check.py`) | does the script load, in the tree and in the official v2.0.32? | **0** gaps in 1745 scripts |
| the sweep of the loads of the v1 (`scripts/sweep-v1-loads.py`) | does the v1.1.37.02 and the tree complain the same about every command? | **257** commands, **0** different |
| the sweep of the loads of the v2 (`scripts/sweep-v2-loads.py`) | does the v2.0.32 and the tree load the same line? | 300 names, 173 differences, **none** of them in a name of the family of the windows |
| the target of the family of the windows (`scripts/measure-window-targets-2.0.32.py`) | does the call that names no window ask about the same window? | **17 of 19** |

The two names of the family that are left are the `WinGetTransparent` and
the `WinGetProcessPath`, and the two need a capacity of the desktop that the
tree does not ask for yet.

## The gaps that are left

There are none of the kind that the tree refuses a script that the official
loads. What is left are the holes of the behaviour, and each one of them
names the measurement that found it:

- the **`WinGetTransparent`** of a window that is not layered: the v2.0.32
  answers *unset* (`lib/win.cpp:1853`), and the tree answers 255;
- the **`WinGetProcessPath`**: the reader of the desktop uses the
  `GetModuleFileNameExW` of the psapi, which wants `PROCESS_VM_READ`; the
  `QueryFullProcessImageNameW` of the kernel32 would answer it, and it is of
  Windows Vista -- the answer is to resolve it at the run, the way the
  `DwmGetWindowAttribute` is resolved;
- the **`FileAppend` to the console**: the output of the tree is a list of
  lines, and the text that a `FileAppend` writes ends with a line break; the
  list keeps the empty line of it, and two writes are separated by a blank
  line that the v2 does not write.

And one of the speed: the text of the arguments of a built-in is still copied,
and it is the workload `strings` that is 1,24x the official.
