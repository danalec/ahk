# The 0.6.3: what the release carries, and the gaps that are left

The record of the release **0.6.3** of the tree. It says what the release
carries, what was measured, and what the tree does not carry yet.

The record of the dialect of the v2.0.32 is in
[`v2.0.32-changes.md`](v2.0.32-changes.md), and the instrument that says what
the tree does not carry is in
[`corpus/v2-corpus-gaps.md`](corpus/v2-corpus-gaps.md).

## What the release carries

The release is the sum of the commits between the 0.6.2 and it, and they are
of three kinds.

**The native call of the v1.** It was the place where the tree was furthest
from the binary of the official release, and eleven commits closed it:

- the text of a call is the buffer of the variable, and not a copy of it;
- the variable that the call writes through a reference is not warned about;
- the value that the call refused is in the `Extra` of the failure;
- the room of the buffer of a text of a call is the room of the variable;
- the call that writes into a variable of the caller needs the capacity of it;
- the `VarSetCapacity` of the v1 fills the variable with the byte;
- the zero of the room of a variable does not free it;
- a negative capacity that is not minus one is a failure;
- the v1 does not refuse a text that is not terminated in the buffer;
- the target of the call may be a variable that holds the name of the
  function, and not only a text;
- the download of the network opens no window of the console.

**The name of a hotkey of the v2.** The name of a hotkey of the v2 is the
text of the line, and the backtick of it is the escape of the character that
follows it and the quote of it is the key of the quote: the
``CapsLock & `;::`` and the `CapsLock & '::` are hotkeys, and the
combination of keys -- the `a & b::` -- is one hotkey with free spaces around
the `&`. The name is the text that comes before the **first** pair of colons
of the line, and it has to be the name of a hotkey: the line
`parts1 := SplitByMultiChar(text1, "::")` is a statement, and the quote of it
opens a text.

**The v1 that a real program asked for.** A program of the v1 with 108
thousand lines found what the corpus of the v2 could not reach: the base of an instance of the v1 is the **class** of
it, and not its prototype; the statics of the chain of the classes are the
statics of the class; the window that a thread destroys does not ask for a
loan, and the thread-local of the tooltip does not panic when the thread
ends.

And three rules of the v2 that the corpus of the upstream found: the list of
the arguments of a call may end with commas, the argument of a directive ends
at the comment of the line, and a hotstring keeps the script alive after its
statements end.

## What was measured

Every number of this release is a measurement against the binary of the
official release, and every one of them can be run again:

| The instrument | What it asks | The answer |
|---|---|---|
| the corpus of the upstream (`scripts/corpus-check.py`) | does the script load, in the tree and in the official v2.0.32? | **0** gaps in 1745 scripts |
| the pairs of the upstream (`scripts/pairs-check.py`) | the same, for the 851 pairs of the v1 and of the v2 | **0** gaps of the v2, **51** of the v1 |
| the differential harness (`scripts/differential.py`) | does the real binary and the tree answer the same? | **114 of 114** MATCH |

## The gaps that are left

They are two lists, and they are of two kinds.

**The fifty-one gaps of the v1**, out of the 851 pairs of the upstream, by
the shape of the refusal: the command of the v1 that the tree does not carry
("This line does not contain a recognized action." in twelve of them), the
call of a function that the v1 has and the tree does not ("Call to
nonexistent function." in nine), and thirty more of fourteen other shapes.

The v1 had sixty-eight gaps and has fifty-one: the name of a label of the
v1 may carry any character, the hotstring of the v1 may carry the options
of the v2, the line that begins with three colons is the hotkey whose name
is the ":", and the commands that turn the lock keys, that write the text
of a control and that undo the minimize of every window are carried.

**The forty-five gaps of the other kind**, out of the 1745 scripts of the
corpus: the tree loads a script that the official v2.0.32 refuses at the
load. They are the rules of the v2 that the tree does not carry yet -- the
name of a class of the script is not the output variable of an assignment
(12), the name of a function of the machine is not either (7), a hotkey is
not allowed inside a function (4), a call does not take more parameters than
the function takes (3), and the other nineteen of five other shapes.

The table of every one of them, with the numbers and the reasons, is in
[`corpus/v2-corpus-gaps.md`](corpus/v2-corpus-gaps.md).

**The six gaps of the v2 that the pairs found are closed**: the name of the
v2 carries any character that is not ASCII, the text that is glued after
the closing percent is a part of the name, at the load any single
character is the name of a key, the line that begins with three colons is
a hotkey and not a hotstring, the name of a property may be built by the
script, and a word of the language that is followed by a percent sign is
the beginning of a name that the script builds.
