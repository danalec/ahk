Port the family of the focused edit, and write the matrix of the desktops to test on

The fourth family of the port (`docs/two-lines.md` §5.2): the edit that has
the focus. The `Edit*` names of v2 read the caret, the line and the
selection of an edit of the window of the script, and the tree of the owner
refused every one of them with the blanket reason of the names of Windows.

The port is the seven handlers and the rows of the table that answer to
them: the capability was already there -- the methods of the `Screen` trait
that answer these questions, and the operations of the helper of the
window (`cursor`, `value`, `lines`, `selection` and `caret`).

**The names take the handle of the control.** `EditGetCurrentLine` and its
kin want one argument at least, and that argument is the handle -- the
number that the helper knows the control by. A call without it fails with
the reason of v2.

**The surface moves with it: 747 answers with a value and 86 are refused
become 754 and 79.**

Measured on the box on 2026-11-11, with this tree built there, and a
window of the script with one `Edit` that holds "hello from the port":
`EditGetCurrentLine(1)` answers 1, `EditGetCurrentCol(1)` answers 20,
`EditGetLineCount(1)` answers 1, `EditGetSelectedText(1)` answers "hello
from the port" and `CaretGetPos()` answers 1.

And the round writes `docs/desktop-test-matrix.md`: what the tree asks of
the desktop (the portable listing of the toplevels, the extension of
COSMIC, the bus of the accessibility, the GTK 3 of the helper, the X11 of
the X sessions), which desktop answers what, and how one VM can carry
several of them -- because the tree asks the compositor *of the session*,
and a change that is proved against one compositor is proved against one
compositor.
