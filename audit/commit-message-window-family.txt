Port the family of the windows of the machine, and merge the two clients of the compositor

The third family of the port (`docs/two-lines.md` §5.2): the windows. The
`Win*` names of v2 move, show and close the windows of the session, and the
tree of the owner refused every one of them with the blanket reason of the
names of Windows.

The port is:

- The three answers the family needs (`WindowRef`, `WindowGeometry` and
  `WaitFor`), the seven methods of the traits that carry it and the
  twenty-eight answers of the box.
- The thirty-four handlers of the family, the helpers they call and the
  rows of the built-in table that answer to them. **A name that the tree of
  the owner answers already is not taken away from it**: `WinGetID`,
  `WinGetList`, `WinGetCount` and `WinGetClass` keep the handler of the
  tree, and the port gives the names that the tree refused.
- The two hooks of the runtime that remember the window that was asked for
  last, so that `WinGetIDLast` answers about it.
- The cases of the family, and the fake of the suite, which answers the
  four questions of the compositor with the windows that the tree of the
  owner already lists.

**And the two clients of the compositor are merged by hand**
(`audit/port-wayland-merge.py`). The client of the tree of the owner
learned to name the windows of the session with a number, and the client
of the branch learned where a window is, in what state it is, and the
scale of an output. Neither is copied: the merge writes the two texts
that the two trees disagree about -- the geometry of a window and the scale
of an output -- and the client of the tree of the owner stays.

**The surface moves with it: 712 answers with a value and 121 are refused
become 747 and 86.**

Measured on the box on 2026-11-11, with this tree built there:
`WinGetCount()` answers 3 and `WinGetList()` answers the ids 1 2 3 -- the
three windows of the session, "Gmail - Chromium", "Unlock Keyring" and
"Steam". `WinWait("zzz-nothing-zzz", "", 1)` answers 0 in 1012 ms, and
`WinGetPos` fails with the reason of the machine -- "the compositor did
not say where this window is, so its place is not known" -- and not with a
zero.
