Port the family of the controls of another window, and the bus that answers it

The second family of the port (`docs/two-lines.md` §5.2): the accessibility
bus. The `Control*` names of v2 read and write the controls of *another*
window, and the tree of the owner refused every one of them with the blanket
reason of the names of Windows.

The port is:

- The module of the bus (`crates/ahk-box/src/atspi.rs`) and the three
  answers it carries: `ControlAnswer`, `ControlFlag` and `ControlEdit`.
- The eight methods of the `Windows` trait that the family needs -- the
  text, the flag, the write, the click, the focus and the pid of the window
  -- and the eight answers of the box that carry them, with the four
  helpers that tell the window of the script from the windows of the
  session.
- The twenty-six handlers of the family, the helpers they call and the
  thirty-two rows of the built-in table that answer to them. Twenty-eight of
  the rows carry a handler now, and the four of the styles keep the refusal
  of the branch, because a style of a control is a thing of Windows.
- The cases of the family are the ones of the branch, and the fake of the
  suite answers them.

**The surface moves with it: 684 values and 149 refusals become 712 and
121.** The twenty-eight names of the family answer with a value, and the
case of the runner that read the blanket reason of `ControlSendText` now
reads the reason of the family.

Measured on the box on 2026-11-11, with this tree built there:
`ControlGetText("Edit1", "the-probe-of-the-controls")` answers "hello from
the port" -- the text of a control of a window, read through the bus of the
accessibility.

And the port is a script and not a copy by hand
(`audit/port-control-family.py` and `audit/port-control-cases.py`): the two
trees have drifted, and the rows of the built-in table are in another order
in each of them.
