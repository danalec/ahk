Answer the tray of the session, and call the callback of a menu item by its value

The icon of the script is in the tray of the session, and a click on it
runs the item by default of the menu of the script. The AHK_X11 has no
tray at all, and this is the piece that carries the tree past it.

The helper of the tray had to serve the bus and not only the runner: the
watcher asks the process for the properties of the item, and it asks it
by the bus, so a helper that waits for the commands of the runner in a
blocking read never answers it and the click is never delivered. The
commands are read by the loop of GLib now, the answer of the helper is
read and understood -- it is JSON, and a helper that stopped answering is
a failure with a reason and not a hang -- and the property of the item is
a variant: the watcher was answered with a failure for every property of
it, and the icon was in the tray with no icon and no title. The helper
lives as long as the desktop does, and the desktop takes it away with it:
a script that ends takes its icon away with it.

And the callback of a menu item is called by its value and not by its
name: a closure has no name in v2, and the tree dropped every handler
that was one -- the callback of a menu item, and the callback of an event
of a window. The binary refuses a callback that cannot take the three
parameters of the callback of a menu item, with "Invalid callback
function.", and the check is at the Add and not when the item is chosen.
The arity of a variadic function is the one of the binary: the parameters
that come before the rest are counted, and the flag of the rest is one.

Verified on the box on 2026-11-11: the item of the script is in the
watcher of the session, and the report of a run whose icon was clicked
says "clicked: &Open".
